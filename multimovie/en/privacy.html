MultiMovie Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TSUBASA FUKUCHI (the "Operator") handles information in the iOS and iPadOS app MultiMovie (the "App").

Information the Operator collects

The Operator has no servers and does not collect your personal information or usage history. The URLs and layouts you enter are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud storage. The Operator cannot see them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App uses AdMob, an ad service from Google LLC. AdMob may collect the following information to serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier)
  • Approximate location (estimated from your IP address)
  • Usage information, such as ads shown and tapped
  • Diagnostics, such as crashes and performance

You can choose whether to allow use of the advertising identifier on a screen shown the first time you use the App, and change it later in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Tracking). If you do not allow it, non-personalized ads are shown. No ads are shown while you use Pro.

In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, a screen asks for your choice about the use of data for advertising the first time you use the App. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Ad Privacy Settings.

For how Google handles data, see Google's policy.

Websites you view

The App displays the websites you specify. Each site's own policy applies to how it handles information. Site data such as cookies is stored on your device and shared among the App's tiles.

Payments

Apple processes subscription payments. The Operator does not receive your payment information.

Children

The App provides unrestricted web access and is not directed to children.
