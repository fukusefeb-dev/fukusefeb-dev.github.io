MultiMovie Help

MultiMovie plays the pages you choose on up to four screens at the same time.

Add to a tile

Tap "Add URL" on a tile and paste a URL, or tap Share in Safari or a video app and choose MultiMovie. After sharing, you can choose a tile the next time you open MultiMovie.

Switch the sound

Tap the speaker in the strip above a tile. Only one tile plays sound at a time, shown with a yellow border. Just after switching, the previous tile's video may stop for a second or two.

Choose a split

Use the button at the left of the top bar to choose single, 2-split, 3-split, 4-split, or 1 large + 3 small. The arrangement follows your screen's orientation so that each video is as large as possible (on an iPhone in portrait, tiles stack vertically). Three or more tiles is a Pro feature; in the free version you can watch an ad to use it for 3 hours.

Move between pages inside a tile

Use Back and Forward in a tile's "…" menu. When you reach the page you want, choose Save Current Page to keep it in the tile.

History and favorites

When editing a tile, choose "Choose from History & Favorites" to pick a URL you used before. History and favorites are stored on that device and are not synced to your other devices.

Share a layout

In the layout list, swipe a row left (or touch and hold) and choose Share to show a link and a QR code. The person who receives it can check the pages before adding the layout to their MultiMovie. The link contains each tile's URL, so make sure the URLs don't include sign-in details before sharing.

Immersive View and Sleep Timer (Pro)

Choose them from "…" in the top bar. Immersive View hides the strips above the tiles to enlarge the video (tap the handle at the top to bring the bar back). Sleep Timer stops all tiles after the time you choose.

Zoom

Double-tap the strip above a tile, or choose Zoom from the "…" menu.

Show on a TV

An HDMI adapter is the most stable. With AirPlay screen mirroring, video may lag or lose quality depending on your connection. While showing on a TV, your phone is the remote. Don't lock your phone. Locking it stops the video. To dim the screen, use Power Saving. If the edges of the picture are cut off, make the display size smaller under TV Display on the remote screen.

If a video doesn't play

  • Choose Reload from the "…" menu.
  • If it still doesn't play, choose Report a Playback Problem from the "…" menu to let us know.
  • Some sites don't support simultaneous playback or embedding. Try Desktop Site.

Pro (subscription)

To cancel or change your plan, open the Settings app and go to Apple Account > Subscriptions. After changing devices, tap Restore Purchases in the app's settings.

iCloud sync

Layouts sync between iPhones and iPads that use the same Apple Account. It may take a little time to update.
